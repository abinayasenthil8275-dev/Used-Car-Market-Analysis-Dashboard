 Used Car Market Analysis

Project Overview
   1.The Used Car Market Analysis project focuses on analyzing used car listings and resale prices to understand market trends and buyer preferences.

   2.This project uses Excel, MySQL, and Power BI to explore car prices, brands, fuel types, transmission types, seller categories, and vehicle age.

   3.The main goal is to provide meaningful business insights that help buyers make better purchasing decisions and sellers understand market pricing trends.

 Project Objectives
- Analyze the overall used car market.
- Identify the average price of different car brands.
- Understand the distribution of cars based on fuel type.
- Analyze the relationship between car age and resale price.
- Compare prices between automatic and manual transmission.
- Identify pricing differences based on seller type.
- Highlight suitable used cars for buyers.

 Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel: Working with tabular data.
- MySQL: Database and SQL queries.
- Power BI: Interactive dashboards and data visualization.

 Key Business Insights

1. Fuel Analysis: Diesel accounts for 54.5% of listings, while Petrol accounts for 44.21%.

2. Brand Analysis: Lexus has the highest displayed average price among the brands shown, followed by Volvo and BMW.

3. Depreciation Analysis: Average car prices generally decrease as vehicle age increases.

4. Transmission Analysis: Automatic cars have a higher displayed average price (1.32M) compared to Manual cars (0.45M).

5. Seller Analysis: Dealer listings show the highest average price, followed by Trustmark Dealers and Individual sellers.

6. Buyer Insights: Cars with lower age and lower kilometers driven can help buyers identify suitable options within their budget.

Conclusion
1.The Used Car Market Analysis project provides a clear understanding of used car pricing trends and market patterns.

2.The dashboards help identify the impact of brand, fuel type, car age, transmission, and seller type on average prices.

3.These insights can support better purchasing decisions for buyers and pricing decisions for sellers.
